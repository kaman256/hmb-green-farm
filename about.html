About HMB Green Farm

Discover our farm, our agricultural activities and the place we call home — Kawango, Mpigi.

Our story

Grounded in agriculture. Growing in Kawango.

HMB Green Farm Kawango is an agricultural farm located in Kawango, Kayabwe Town Council, Mpigi District, Uganda. The farm brings together dairy cattle, livestock rearing, poultry and crop production.

Our photographs tell the story of a real working environment: animal housing, cattle feeding areas, goats, green fodder and open farm grounds. We welcome enquiries from people interested in learning more about our activities and the farm.

Our approach

Practical farming, every day.

A farm is more than its buildings. It is the animals, the land, the people and the daily work that brings everything together. At HMB Green Farm, these elements are part of a diverse agricultural operation.
