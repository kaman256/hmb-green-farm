Welcome to HMB Green Farm

A farm built on care, hard work and growth.

At HMB Green Farm Kawango, agriculture is hands-on. Our farm brings together animal rearing, dairy operations and crop growing in one place, with a focus on responsible farm management and practical agricultural production.

  • Dairy cattle and livestock management
  • Goat rearing and poultry farming
  • Fodder cultivation and crop production
  • Farm enquiries and visits by arrangement
